1. B-Roll

Supplementary or alternative footage filmed to illustrate events as they occur, build geographic or situational context, and visually cover cuts made during interviews.

While primary footage often consists of talking heads, B-roll provides the visual texture of the environment. It captures unscripted actions, atmospheric details, and everyday routines that allow viewers to feel physically immersed in the world of the documentary.
